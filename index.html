1. Mechanical Stress & Elastic Boundary Coupling

Hooke-Lattice Continuum

Prismatic battery cell swelling inside a rigid structural containment casing generates an internal normal stress $\sigma_{\text{stack}}(t)$ that translates directly into tensile bolt force $F(t)$. Total strain $\varepsilon_{\text{total}}$ is divided into elastic compliance, reversible intercalation strain $\varepsilon_{\text{inter}}$, and irreversible plastic creep $\varepsilon_{\text{creep}}$:

$$\sigma_{\text{stack}}(t) = E_{\text{eff}} \left[ \varepsilon_{\text{total}} - \left( \varepsilon_{\text{inter}}(Q, T) + \varepsilon_{\text{SEI}}(t) + \varepsilon_{\text{dead-Li}}(N) \right) \right]$$

The total normal force $F$ exerted on the titanium-aluminum tie-rod restraint frame with cross-sectional contact area $A_{\text{cell}}$ and structural compliance $K_{\text{frame}}$ follows:

$$F(Q, N) = F_0 + K_{\text{pack}} \cdot \left( \sum_{i=1}^{M} \Delta L_{\text{rev}, i}(Q) + \Delta L_{\text{irrev}, i}(N) \right)$$
$E_{\text{eff}} = 1.42\text{ GPa}$ (Porous Electrode) $K_{\text{pack}} = 28.4\text{ kN/mm}$

2. Microscopic Intercalation & Dead Li Deposition

Crystallographic Phase Drift

Lithium insertion into graphite active material forces a stage transition from dilute Stage $4$ ($\text{LiC}_{72}$) through Stage $1$ ($\text{LiC}_6$), expanding the graphite $c$-axis unit cell by $\approx 13.2\%$. Under lunar sub-zero charging ($-20^\circ\text{C}$ to $-40^\circ\text{C}$), the charge-transfer resistance $R_{\text{ct}}$ spikes exponentially per the Butler-Volmer relation:

$$j_{\text{plating}} = j_0 \left[ \exp\left( \frac{\alpha_a F \eta_{\text{over}}}{RT} \right) - \exp\left( -\frac{\alpha_c F \eta_{\text{over}}}{RT} \right) \right], \quad \eta_{\text{over}} = \Phi_s - \Phi_l - U_{\text{Li/Li}^+}$$

When the local overpotential $\eta_{\text{over}} < 0$, metallic lithium plates onto the graphite surface rather than intercalating. Subsequent reaction with alkyl carbonates produces non-passivating mossy dendrites, creating an unyielding permanent volumetric swelling thickness $\delta_{\text{dead-Li}}$:

$$\delta_{\text{dead-Li}}(t) = \int_{0}^{t} \frac{\bar{V}_{\text{Li}}}{z F_F \cdot A_{\text{cell}}} \cdot I_{\text{plating}}(\tau) \, d\tau + k_{\text{SEI}} \sqrt{t} \cdot e^{-\frac{E_a}{R T}}$$
$\bar{V}_{\text{Li}} = 13.02\text{ cm}^3/\text{mol}$ $E_a = 48.2\text{ kJ/mol}$ (Solvent Red.)

3. Baseline Inversion & Force-Charge Estimator

Kalman-Augmented Observer

Standard Battery Management Systems (BMS) cannot separate capacity loss caused by active material cracking from dead lithium deposition. The creep tracker isolates the irreversible baseline offset $F_0(N)$ by executing a recursive least-squares (RLS) inversion across the full charge cycle $(Q_k, F_k)$:

$$\hat{F}(Q_k) = \hat{F}_0 + \kappa_1 Q_k + \kappa_2 Q_k^2 + \Delta F_{\text{hyst}}(Q_k, \text{sign}(\dot{Q}_k))$$

When the state observer extracts $\hat{F}_0 > F_{\text{baseline, nominal}}$, the autonomous closed-loop firmware derates the maximum charge voltage cut-off ($V_{\text{max}}$) and charge capacity limit ($Q_{\text{ceiling}}$) to prevent containment housing plastic deformation:

$$Q_{\text{ceiling}}(t) = Q_{\text{nominal}} \cdot \max \left( 0.40, \; 1.0 - \beta \left[ \frac{\hat{F}_0(t) - F_{\text{nominal}}}{F_{\text{yield}} - F_{\text{nominal}}} \right]^2 \right)$$
